Hiace, Bolero, night buses: what the notice says
  • Hiace, minibus, micro bus, jeep (Bolero): the department gives one fare list for public passenger vehicles and names bus, minibus, micro bus and jeep in its deluxe rules. It gives no separate Hiace or Bolero fare. If one runs a listed public route, ask the department which class applies.
  • Deluxe and super deluxe: a vehicle may add 20% for deluxe, and another 20% on the deluxe fare for super deluxe, only after the department's test office has certified that class.
  • Night buses: the notice has no night surcharge. It only requires two drivers on long-distance night buses.
  • Inside Gandaki Province: jeeps Rs 7 per km, buses Rs 4 per km (province notice, May 2026).

Was the app fare high?

There is no final official fare for ride apps yet. A cap of Rs 25/km for bikes and Rs 55/km for cars has been proposed but is a draft (Kathmandu Post).

Discounts

  • All concession classes. The department's notice of 16 Baisakh 2083 says students, people with disabilities, people disabled in the people's movement and senior citizens must get a concession at the prescribed rate, on showing the original ID, with no limit on route, quota, day or time. It does not state the rate. Official notice
  • Students: 45% (reported). The department wrote to operators in Baisakh 2083 (April 2026) to enforce it, based on the 2065-11-20 BS decision. Source is a news report, the letter itself was not opened. Nonstop Khabar
  • Senior citizens: at least 50% (reported). Senior Citizens Act 2063, section 9: two reserved seats and at least 50% off. On 26 Shrawan 2083 (11 Aug 2026) the social security ministry asked the transport ministry to enforce it. The 2070 procedure is reported to apply it to the two reserved seats. Onlinekhabar
  • People with disabilities. The discount must be given but no rate was found, so none is applied here.

How the numbers are made

  • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Small local stops use exact bus-stop positions from OpenStreetMap, and Gairigaun Petrol Pump uses its Google Maps position. Distances come from OpenStreetMap routing between the two places you type. A conductor's distance may differ a little, so near a price step we show both fares.
  • Taxi figures are estimates from the official meter rates. A real meter counts the route driven and waiting time.
  • Ride-app prices are read by hand from each app's own price screen at a recorded time. Uber is shown at its standard price, before personal promotions.
  • We use areas only. No names, phone numbers or number plates. We are independent of every ride app.
